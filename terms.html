Refund policy and terms

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What you are buying

Membership in Journey, a structured program for professionals rebuilding after a layoff, guided personally by Tad Natsuhara. You move through three phases (Reground, Reorient and Rebuild) at your own pace. Membership includes a 45-minute one-to-one call in your first week and a daily check-in that I read. You start on your own, and there is no group to wait for.

Journey is new and still being built in the open. The refund terms below exist because of that.

Price and billing

  • $49 CAD per month, charged when you start and on the same date each month after that.
  • This is a subscription. It renews automatically until you cancel.
  • If the price ever changes, you will be told before your next charge.

Refunds

  • If Journey is not right for you, you are refunded in full if you say so within fourteen days of your first payment. Reply to any email from Journey. There is no form and there are no conditions.
  • Refunds are issued through Stripe to the original payment method and typically appear within five to ten business days depending on your bank.
  • Outside that window, refunds are at my discretion, and I will be generous rather than technical about it. Ask.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time by emailing the address below. Cancelling stops future charges, and your access continues until the end of the month you have already paid for.

What Journey is not

Journey is a structured, guided program. It is not therapy, counselling, medical care, or clinical treatment, and it is not legal or financial advice. It is not an employment or placement service and no job outcome is promised. If what you are carrying needs professional support, tell me and I will help you find it.

Payment processing and your data

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's own hosted checkout and are never collected, transmitted, or stored by this website. Journey receives your name, email address, and the fact of payment.

Your email is used to run the program and to tell you about Journey. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Ask at any time and your data will be deleted.
