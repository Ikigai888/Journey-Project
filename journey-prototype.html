Week 5 · Reorient

Rewrite the story.

The version of what happened that's true, shame-free, and you can say out loud without your chest tightening.

This week

Monday check-in
Read: why the story you tell matters
Write the first draft of your story
Say it out loud once, to anyone
Thursday call with Tad

Not this week

No applications. No LinkedIn rewrite. No "what's next" conversations with people who'll ask about salary.

You'll get there in week 9. Doing it now, before the story lands, is how people end up describing a job they don't want.

Phase-aware pacing
Why this order? Identity work before search work isn't a nicety. The story you tell about the layoff becomes the story you tell in interviews. Get it honest first, and the search gets easier.

When it's heavier than this

Journey is a structure, not therapy. Tad is a guide who's been through a layoff — not a therapist or counsellor.

If what you're carrying needs professional care, that's not the program failing. It's the program working.

Your former employer's EAP may still cover sessions after a layoff. Most people don't know this. We'll help you check.

In crisis? If you're thinking about harming yourself, please don't wait for Thursday. 9-8-8 — Suicide Crisis Helpline (call or text, 24/7). Tad is notified when this page is opened, and will reach out — but a trained human is available right now.
What the guide is: someone who asks the same questions in the same order, notices patterns across weeks, holds you to what you said you'd do, and knows when to point you somewhere else. What the guide isn't: a clinician. Both things are said out loud on day one.
